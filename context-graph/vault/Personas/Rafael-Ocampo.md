# Rafael Ocampo

*Constraint persona (design stress-test)*

**Role:** Warehouse night shift, 61. Rents a two-bedroom.  
**Tech comfort:** Low. Phone only, no computer, no printer, storage nearly full.  

## Their words

> "Tell me the number I have to pay and the day. Everything else is noise until I know that."

## What this persona breaks

Language. Photo-only capture. Arrears versus usage. The entire alert tone.

## Why this matters for design

Rafael sets the bar for the hardest case. Low tech, cracked screen, standing at 6am. If the app works for him, it works for everyone. His scam history means we never use alarm-colour or countdown timers — ever.
