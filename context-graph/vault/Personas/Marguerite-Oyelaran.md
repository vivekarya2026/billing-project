# Marguerite Oyelaran

*Constraint persona (design stress-test)*

**Role:** Retired school administrator, 74. Lives alone.  
**Tech comfort:** Low to medium. iPad daily for messages and photos. Reading glasses.  

## Their words

> "I don't need a chart. I need someone to tell me if I'm alright."

## What this persona breaks

Type size. Target size. Any sentence needing a glossary. Any flow requiring trust.

## Why this matters for design

Marguerite is not an edge case — she is the floor. If a target is too small for her, it annoys Dana too. Design for Marguerite and Dana gets a better product for free. No accessibility mode. The default IS accessible.
