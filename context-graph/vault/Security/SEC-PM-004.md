# SEC-PM-004

**Category:** authorization  
**Owner:** architect  

## What to watch out for

Users may add bills in another person's name, legitimate for renters and family. We must never verify ownership, and must never allow this to become a surveillance mechanism.

> **Note:** These items are identified, not solved. The architect owns the solution.
