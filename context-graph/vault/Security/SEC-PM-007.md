# SEC-PM-007

**Category:** data_protection  
**Owner:** architect  

## What to watch out for

Encrypted local backup becomes the single point of data loss and the single most valuable artifact to steal. Key handling is critical.

> **Note:** These items are identified, not solved. The architect owns the solution.
